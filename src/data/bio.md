> *"If you can't go a day without thinking about something, don't let a day pass where you don't work towards it."*

I am a **Computer Scientist** at the **Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC Corona)** and a **Department of Defense (DoD) SMART Scholar**, where I develop **automated computer vision impact-scoring systems**, **missile test data analysis software**, and **NISE-funded AI agent regression testing frameworks**. I earned my **B.S. in Computer Science, *Magna Cum Laude*, from Cal Poly Pomona (3.89 GPA)**. There, I conducted research in the **Reconfigurable Space Computing Lab (RSCL)**, developing:
* **SOAR:** 3D autonomous drone obstacle avoidance via **Deep Q-Networks (DQN)** and **PPO**.
* **N.E.A.T. Simulations:** Neuroevolutionary 2D flight simulations.
* **D.E.F.E.C.T.:** A framework for testing **Reinforcement Learning (RL)** effectiveness in combinatorial tasks and explosive action spaces.

At the heart of my passion for computer science is **evolution and simulation**. Growing up, I watched countless videos from **[Code Bullet](https://youtube.com/@codebullet?si=v7DpDrVm86-6A7Cp)** and similar creators on **genetic algorithms** and training AI agents to master complex environments. When choosing my university major, I knew immediately: *"I just want to go into whatever that was."* Along the way—from being selected as a **NASA Community College Aerospace Scholar (NCAS)** to diving deep into Sutton & Barto's *Reinforcement Learning* and Seacord's *Effective C*—I built strong complementary foundations in **computer graphics, physics simulations, robotics, and computational game theory**. This culminated in engineering **[Goopis](https://github.com/KELECHIDAVIS/Goopis)**, my **C-based minimax chess engine** that has played over **28,000 games on Lichess**. I love watching systems adapt, improve, and discover **emergent strategies** by leveraging compute to fast-forward thousands of agent years.

In community college, I realized I could pursue these exact passions professionally through academic research. Having presented my work at the **Naval Applications of Machine Learning (NAML) 2026 Conference**, the **NSBE 2026 Ghana Research Showcase**, and the **NSBE 2025 Fall Regional Conference**, my goal is to join a **Ph.D. research lab (applying for Fall 2028 matriculation)** focused on **high-throughput simulation, reinforcement learning, neuroevolution, physics-based graphics, simulated robotics, and game-theoretic decision-making**—while inspiring the next generation of young researchers along the way.
