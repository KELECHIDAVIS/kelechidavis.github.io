I'm a Computer Scientist at the **Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC Corona)** and a **DoD SMART Scholar**, building computer vision scoring systems, missile test data analysis software, and AI agent testing frameworks. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science, *Magna Cum Laude*, from **Cal Poly Pomona** (3.89 GPA), where my research in the **Reconfigurable Space Computing Lab** included **SOAR**, a 3D drone obstacle avoidance system built on Deep Q-Networks and PPO.

Growing up, I watched countless [Code Bullet](https://www.youtube.com/@CodeBullet) videos on genetic algorithms and AI agents learning to master complex environments. When it was time to choose a major, I thought, *“I just want to go into whatever that was.”* That curiosity grew into computer graphics, physics simulations, robotics, and game theory, like [Goopis](https://github.com/KELECHIDAVIS/Goopis), my C-based minimax chess engine with over 12,000 Lichess games. I love seeing systems adapt, improve, and discover emergent strategies, leveraging compute to fast-forward thousands of agent years.

In community college, I realized I could pursue these exact passions through academic research. Having presented at **NAML 2026** and the **NSBE 2026 Ghana Research Showcase**, I'm applying for **Fall 2028** Ph.D. programs in reinforcement learning, neuroevolution, and simulated robotics, while inspiring the next generation of young researchers along the way.
